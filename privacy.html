Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information NextFinds Daily ("we," "us," or "the site") collects when you visit, and how that information is used. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to browse this site. We may collect limited, non-identifying information automatically, such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Browser type, device type, and general location (city/region level)
  • Referring website or search terms that led you here

If you contact us directly by email, we'll have whatever information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address and name.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use cookies or similar technologies from analytics providers to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so may affect how some parts of the site behave.

We do not currently use any third-party analytics or advertising service beyond what is described in this policy. This section will be updated if that changes.

Affiliate Links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, the retailer may share information about that transaction with us (typically limited to the fact that a referred sale occurred) so that we can earn a commission. We do not receive your payment details, and the retailer's own privacy policy governs any information you provide to them directly.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Understand which content is useful so we can improve it
  • Maintain the security and performance of the site
  • Respond to messages sent to us directly

We do not sell personal information.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you've emailed us and would like your message or information removed from our records, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect once posted on this page, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly.
